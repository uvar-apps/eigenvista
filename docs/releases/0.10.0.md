# EigenVista 0.10.0 — room for the molecule

Release prepared 17 November 2026. This update combines the approved
workspace redesign with the scientific-inspection work recorded at the local
0.9.0 checkpoint. The rebuilt Apple Silicon Mac package includes these features,
the converter, examples, palettes and a prebuilt AI connector. It is ad-hoc signed
and not Apple-notarized. Download from the
[0.10.0 release page](https://github.com/uvar-apps/eigenvista/releases/tag/v0.10.0).
Use the project concept DOI until a version-specific DOI is supplied.

Delivered through [PR #89](https://github.com/uvar-apps/eigenvista/pull/89),
merged as `8ab5a0a1e12f7314c93b29745ea75e4c4f11b784`; the annotated
[`v0.10.0` source tag](https://github.com/uvar-apps/eigenvista/tree/v0.10.0)
and main are pushed. The updated website is published and its 89 public files
match the qualified local output. This later delivery record preserves the
immutable tag and its pre-publication notes.

## One pane, four tabs

- A resizable tools pane replaces two narrow permanent sidebars. Choose left or
  right, and use **Molecule**, **Fields**, **Surfaces** and **Appearance** to
  switch tasks. Wide panes let widgets distribute across two columns.
- Hide and reopen the pane, or enter Zen mode for a minimal interface. Browser
  fullscreen remains separate, with visible exit controls.
- Keep familiar collapsible, draggable widgets and keyboard/menu reordering.
  Tab changes, resizing and hiding preserve camera, drafts and accepted results;
  ongoing scientific work retains progress and cancellation.
- Original file actions, status bars, camera, background and image-export
  controls remain in place. Existing native files stay readable.

## Select, measure and inspect molecular shape

Ordered atom picks support distance, angle and signed torsion measurements.
Keep named selections, editable labels, measurement monitors and individual
ligand neighborhoods, export measurement CSV, and include annotations in PNGs.

Generate SES/SAS molecular surfaces with explicit radii, probe size, spacing
and selected-source semantics. Save cached meshes and their provenance. Saved
surface color and opacity update live; renaming is separate. Six symmetric
camera-following lights give balanced built-in illumination when orbiting.

## Read numbers from quantum fields

Open **Fields → Quantitative field inspection** for point probes,
two-dimensional planes, contours and line profiles. Planes can follow coordinate
axes or three ordered atoms, with a continuous color bar and numerical range.
Export original values and coordinates as CSV, or keep the analysis cache in a
native document. Sampling is independent of the three-dimensional preview grid.

Orbital phase plots show signed amplitude ψ. Electron density is nonnegative;
choose the density field explicitly. Unknown cube meanings and units remain
unspecified until declared; samples outside a field's domain remain missing.

## External AI access

Selections, measurements, labels, neighborhoods, molecular surfaces and field
analysis are available through the shared controller and installed AI helper,
including edits, readback, cancellation, save/reopen and exports. User guides
and website tutorials use ordinary-language requests. Workspace placement and
Zen remain GUI preferences; no new layout connector methods are claimed.

## Availability and limits

Use the 0.10.0 Mac package for the new features. The older 0.8.0 app predates
them and rejects documents requiring the new scientific-inspection capabilities.
Earlier documents, frozen scientific inputs and legacy field hashes are
preserved. Dependencies, compiler pins, format identifiers and icon/logo are
unchanged. Palette, background, AO and shader choices remain session settings.

Van der Waals spheres can intersect SES at contact patches; use Ball and stick
or Liquorice for a clearer overlay. The separate pending-inspection/GUI-Save
coordination limitation remains tracked in #85. Qualification is local macOS;
Linux awaits the planned local hosts. WebKit fullscreen entry/exit is verified
inside the rebuilt Mac app on macOS 27.0/arm64; native green-button fullscreen,
detached windows and multi-monitor use remain unqualified.

See [handoff](../handoff.md) and [status](../status.md) for exact fresh
verification results, source identity, PR and delivery state. Historical
candidate results are retained separately and do not qualify the new default.
