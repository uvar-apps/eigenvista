# EigenVista 0.10.4

EigenVista 0.10.4 adds optional ambient occlusion to solvent-excluded (SES)
and solvent-accessible (SAS) molecular surfaces. The matte shading makes
pockets and clefts easier to see when you rotate a large molecule.

## Use surface ambient occlusion

1. Generate an SES or SAS in **Surfaces → Molecular surfaces**.
2. Expand the saved surface and set its opacity to **1**. Use built-in shading.
3. In **Appearance**, enable **Molecular surface ambient occlusion**.
4. Leave **Automatic AO radius** on to scale the radius with the molecule, or
   turn it off to adjust the radius yourself.

The radius defaults to 4 Å for 1CRN and 24 Å for the full 1AON complex. Surface
AO uses Lambert shading without specular reflections. It is independent of
the **Atom and bond ambient occlusion** switch. Orbital and density surfaces,
custom shaders and translucent molecular surfaces use their own shading.
PNG export includes the effect. You can also ask a connected AI agent to set
an SES or SAS to full opacity and turn on surface ambient occlusion.

has an Apple Silicon Mac ZIP and a CachyOS Linux x86_64 tarball, each with a

not notarized it. The Linux preview was built on CachyOS x86_64 with glibc
2.44, GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1. A graphical display is required for the app
and AI connector. The converter can run over SSH. See the [Linux guide](../linux.md)

## Validation and citation

The feature was reviewed on the full 1AON SES with 58,870 atoms and
4,182,788 triangles. The merged feature passed all 758 Chromium and WebKit
cases per platform on macOS and CachyOS. See the [handoff](../handoff.md)
for build and delivery evidence.

Cite this GitHub release for the 0.10.4 source and binaries. The
[Zenodo concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24246123) identifies
EigenVista across releases.
