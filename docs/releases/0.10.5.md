# EigenVista 0.10.5

## Protein coloring by residue class

Open a protein and choose **Molecule → Molecular data → Biological structure →
Coloring → Residue class**. The legend identifies nonpolar, polar uncharged,
acidic and basic residues. Unclassified protein residues appear gray, and
ligands retain element colors. The colors apply to atomic views, traces, tubes,
ribbons and cartoons. You can also ask a connected AI agent to “show the
protein as a cartoon colored by residue class.”

## X-ray shading that follows surface color

Both packages include `surface-x-ray.vert`, `surface-x-ray.frag` and
`surface-x-ray.params` under `Shaders/`. Load the three files in
**Appearance → Surface shader** and keep **Shader transparency** enabled.
The tint updates when you change the surface color. Positive and negative
orbital surfaces keep their individual phase colors. See the close-up of
crambin's solvent-excluded surface in the
[Gallery](https://uvar-apps.github.io/eigenvista/gallery.html).
