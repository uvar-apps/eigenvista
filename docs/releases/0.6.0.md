# EigenVista 0.6.0

EigenVista 0.6.0 is a development preview for Apple Silicon Macs. It adds optional
**molecular screen-space ambient occlusion** to
make folds, cavities and contacts easier to read in large, dense structures.
[#59](https://github.com/uvar-apps/eigenvista/issues/59) · [PR #60](https://github.com/uvar-apps/eigenvista/pull/60)

- Enable **Appearance → Ambient occlusion** for atoms and bonds, including
  space-filling Van der Waals representations. Active AO uses **Lambert shading
  plus SSAO**, without molecular specular highlights.
- Adjust **AO strength**, or keep **Automatic AO radius** enabled to select a
  neighborhood from molecular size. Turn Auto off for manual 0.25–50 Å control.
- Export PNG images with the current shading. AO settings are session-only,
  independently of quantum-chemistry data and saved molecular documents.
- Disable AO or set zero strength to restore the normal molecular view and
  release the effect’s owned resources. Reported enhancement failures retain
  normal rendering with a visible error.

The full 58,870-atom 1AON structure was the primary visual qualification input.
The [design record](https://github.com/uvar-apps/eigenvista/blob/v0.6.0/docs/design/2026-11-14-molecular-ambient-occlusion.md) describes
molecule-only shading, bounded render targets and failure handling. Quantum
surfaces, custom shaders and sampled fields retain their own rendering paths.

This version retains 0.5.15 sidebar reordering and the earlier volume
visibility/depth fix. The Surface shading selector continues to control built-in
saved and raycast isosurfaces; it is separate from molecular AO.

No molecular-file migration is required. Scientific profiles, precision and
allocation limits, dependency/toolchain pins and icon/logo remain unchanged.
Whole-ray volume/opaque intersections and native-save/ExFAT limitations remain.
AO is an approximate screen-space visual cue, not a computed chemistry value.

This remains an experimental development preview with ad-hoc tester signing and
no notarization. Native physical interactions, clean-Mac/HiDPI installation,
Linux/Windows distributions, general scientific/rendering qualification and
power-loss durability remain separate gates. [Status](https://github.com/uvar-apps/eigenvista/blob/v0.6.0/docs/status.md#source-version-060)
records verification, packaging and publication state; changing source versions
does not update an existing installed app or ZIP.
