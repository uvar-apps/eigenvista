EigenVista 0.10.1 · Scientific inspection. The 0.10.1 Mac download includes the four-tab workspace and the scientific-inspection controls described here, through both the GUI and its bundled AI connector. If you are using an older app, update it before following these workflows.

Go from a molecular picture to a scientific observation. Select the atoms, measure their geometry, inspect molecular shape, and put numbers on a field.

Select source atoms in a useful order

Enable Pick atoms for inspection and click atoms, or enter the viewer's atom numbers. Numbers shown to you start at 1. The ordered list has earlier/later and remove buttons. Filtering a chain or changing a representation keeps the original atom identities.

Focus selection frames a set; Show only selection isolates it; Show full structure clears conflicting visibility filters. Save a Named selection when you want to reuse the same atoms for a neighborhood or molecular surface. Renaming or deleting a set never changes the loaded coordinates.

Active picks/highlights are temporary and reset when another source is opened. Named sets are saved with the document. A saved set refers to its original atoms, rather than silently rebinding to similar labels in a different molecule.

Source preview after 0.10.1 — visible picking cues. Gold hollow reticles identify selected atoms. With selection picking off, a normal click shows a cyan ring at the inspected atom or bond endpoints without replacing your selection. Gold takes precedence for atoms already selected. Markers identify source-atom positions, including behind scene geometry; they do not trace atom or molecular-surface boundaries. Hidden atoms stay unmarked. Clear selection removes both cues; opening a new source resets them. Exported PNGs include the markers. The published 0.10.1 download predates this correction.

Measure and annotate

  • Distance: two distinct atoms; readout in Å.
  • Angle: three atoms, centered at the middle atom; readout in degrees.
  • Signed torsion: four ordered atoms; right-handed convention from −180° inclusive to below +180°. Pick order matters.

Choose the kind under Geometry measurements, pick the required atoms and choose Add measurement. Edit the name, input order, kind, color or placement later. Hide a monitor to keep its definition without showing it on the figure.

These are nonperiodic measurements of the supplied source coordinates. Distinct coincident atoms have zero distance; a zero angle arm or degenerate torsion plane is explicitly Undefined, with a reason. The app does not invent a zero or an uncertainty estimate.

Atom and residue labels adds sparse text to a chosen target. Depth-aware labels follow scene occlusion; the always-visible option is useful for a presentation figure. Labels and measurement monitors are included in opaque and transparent PNGs. Export measurement CSV keeps names, ordered inputs, values, units and undefined statuses.

Choose one ligand copy, then its environment

In Ligands and neighborhoods, search the ligand list and choose a particular copy. Chain, residue number, insertion code and displayed residue number distinguish same-name copies. Choose a radius, optionally Include complete residues, then Show neighborhood. Give the result a name to retain it.

The radius is an inclusive atom-center distance from the seeds to all loaded source atoms, including hidden atoms. Seeds remain included. Whole-residue expansion includes all atoms of a matched residue, even if some lie outside the radius; it requires biological identity metadata. The view resets conflicting filters before focusing the result.

This is geometric proximity, not a hydrogen-bond assignment, affinity score, functional-pocket prediction or periodic crystal contact calculation.

Distinguish SAS and SES

A solvent-accessible surface (SAS) traces the solvent probe's center around atomic spheres. A solvent-excluded surface (SES) follows the molecular boundary accessible to the rolling probe, including reentrant shape. They need coordinates and radii, not a wavefunction.

Choose all source atoms, the current selection or a named selection. All includes hidden atoms. A subset is calculated as an isolated object: omitted atoms no longer occlude it. It is not a cropped patch of the full-molecule surface.

Choose Bondi / Mantina or Alvarez radii and explicit probe radius/grid spacing in Å. Radius overrides are available. Missing element radii cause an explicit error; choose a supported table or supply an appropriate radius rather than expecting a generic fallback. Display spheres and covalent-bond radii are separate inputs.

Smaller spacing produces finer geometry and needs more memory. Cavities are kept; spacing is not silently coarsened. Generation has progress and cancellation, and regeneration replaces a cached mesh only when successful. Saved controls retain source/probe/radii/engine provenance, visibility, color and opacity. Surface shading and the X-ray/clipping shaders apply; atom/bond ambient occlusion stays separate.

Expand a saved surface to change its color and opacity immediately, without applying settings or regenerating its mesh. Use Rename surface to commit a name separately. Empty or out-of-range opacity input keeps the last accepted appearance; the generation form's initial color and opacity apply when generating a mesh.

For a clear SES overlay, choose Ball and stick or Liquorice. Van der Waals spheres share the SES contact boundary; their independently triangulated surfaces can cross and produce patchy interference. Opacity does not separate those boundaries. Generation preserves your chosen molecular representation and display radii.

A molecular-shape surface is not electron density or electrostatic potential. Save retains its cached mesh without requiring its original input file or another calculation on reopen.

Source preview after 0.10.1 — live surface progress. The surface controls and workspace activity display show the phase and elapsed time. During mesh generation, a bar shows completed blocks and the current phase's percentage. Other phases show activity without guessing overall completion or remaining time. The bar can restart when the phase changes. Progress and Cancel calculation stay visible when Tools is hidden or Zen is active; cancellation retains the previous accepted surface. Your external AI assistant can report the same status. The published 0.10.1 download predates these detailed progress displays.

Give a field an honest meaning

Open Fields → Quantitative field inspection, choose the analysis field and expand Field meaning and units. Known analytic orbitals/density matrices have their supported interpretation; cube values remain unspecified until a meaning is supplied. Knowing the coordinate unit or filename does not identify the scalar quantity.

An orbital section shows the signed amplitude ψ: positive and negative values identify phase. Total electron density ρ = Σ ni|ψi|² is nonnegative and uses density units. Choose the total-density field for a density section; changing a label or colormap does not square an orbital. Spin or difference densities can have either sign and must retain their own meaning.

Apply field meaning records a user declaration. Declaring source units labels the existing values without rescaling them. Supported Display and export unit conversions change readouts/keys/CSV while keeping raw samples intact. Orbital sign colors represent phase, not positive and negative charge.

Read points, planes and lines

A Point probe uses an entered coordinate or one selected atom. Plane / contours uses XY/XZ/YZ or three ordered atoms, with explicit width/height and columns/rows. The first atom centers the plane, the second defines its horizontal direction, and the third fixes its orientation. Collinear choices are rejected. A Line profile uses two endpoints or two ordered atoms, includes both endpoints and plots distance in Å.

Coordinate inputs accept Å or bohr. Values come from direct analytic evaluation or interpolation of the original scalar grid, independent of the 3D preview's resolution. Skewed/reflected grid axes are preserved. Outside-domain samples are visibly missing, not zero-filled or extrapolated; profiles have gaps and contours do not bridge invalid cells.

Use View meshes and analyses when a 3D volume hides a slice. This changes the viewing mode without generating a mesh. Existing saved meshes keep their own visibility; hide them separately if needed. Return explicitly to volume/raycast when you want that preview again.

Each result supports Edit sampling, Recompute, visibility, label, colormap and range choices. A fixed range is useful for comparisons; Automatic sampled range restores the sampled range. Enter comma-separated plane Contour levels, then Apply result settings.

Source preview after 0.10.1 — automatic plane updates. A newly created plane stays open for editing with Auto-update plane enabled. Use a sampling spinner or its arrow keys; press Enter after typing a value. Orientation changes update it too. Rapid changes use the newest valid definition, keeping the previous complete plane and contours visible while work runs. Turn automatic updates off to edit several values before choosing Recompute analysis. Use Cancel plane update or Cancel editing to stop pending automatic work; collapsing the widget or hiding Tools keeps it running. Coordinate display-unit changes alone do not resample. The published 0.10.1 download uses manual recomputation.

Use Show numeric key for a quantitative slice's continuous colorbar. Show current field rendering key is different: it identifies the actual signed isovalues of mesh/raycast or the two-color volume's opacity transfer. It does not invent a continuous linear scale for those modes.

Numerical samples and provenance and Export analysis CSV keep coordinates, values/units, domain status, method and source identity. CSV reports coordinates/profile distances in Å and values in the chosen display/export unit. Finite numerical data can remain inspectable/exportable when a particular 3D depiction reaches its graphics-precision limit; read the displayed explanation.

Keep the scientific result

Save retains named selections, label and measurement definitions, SES/SAS meshes with provenance, and accepted numerical definitions, caches, interpretation and local appearance. Reopening restores cached fields and meshes without silently recomputing them. Use the current 0.10.1 app; these document capabilities were introduced in 0.10.0 and are not readable by older 0.8.0 packages.

Camera, active pick mode, biological filters, palette, background, AO and custom shader settings remain session choices. Save the document and export the figure separately. PNGs include scientific labels/monitors/visible keys; toolbar/status overlays stay out.

Plan larger calculations

EigenVista attempts the requested allocations for molecular meshes, named sets, annotations, numerical samples and exports, without fixed memory or item-count quotas. Larger grids and finer surface spacing can require substantially more memory and time. Generation runs in the background with progress and cancellation; recoverable allocation failures retain earlier accepted results.

Requested spacing, cavities and source atoms are preserved. The app does not silently coarsen a surface, shorten a numerical result or discard warnings to make it fit. If your computer cannot allocate a request, choose an explicitly smaller extent/count or a coarser spacing and retry. Actual address-space/index representation, floating-point precision and graphics-device capabilities still apply; read the reported cause.
