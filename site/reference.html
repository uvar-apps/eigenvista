03 / EIGENVISTA FIELD MANUAL

User reference.

EigenVista displays molecular structures and supplied quantum-chemistry fields in an interactive 3D scene. This reference covers EigenVista 0.10.3, including the four-tab workspace and scientific inspection. The 0.10.2 Mac download includes these tools and its AI connector. Start with the installation guide or a worked tutorial with a downloadable example.

New in 0.10.3: action buttons stand out from labels; gold and cyan markers identify selected and inspected atoms; field planes update as you commit sampling changes; and molecular-surface generation shows its phase, elapsed time and measured mesh progress. These additions are being prepared for release; the published download remains 0.10.2. Use the updated inspection tools →

Find a workflow

  • Files, imports, conversion, and saving — choose a format, review import decisions, use the batch converter, and share a self-contained document.
  • Scene, appearance, and images — navigate, pick atoms, choose a representation, use ambient occlusion and palettes, export PNGs, or apply a surface shader.
  • Custom surface shaders — see through a density envelope, expose its interior with clipping, and tune lighting and transparency live.
  • Orbitals, densities, and sampled fields — generate signed surfaces, browse several orbitals, compare render modes, and understand resolution and caching.
  • Biological structures — color chains and secondary structure, show components, isolate a chain, select residue ranges, and use protein depictions.
  • AI-assisted viewing — connect your external coding agent, describe a molecular view, and keep exported figures and native documents.
  • Scientific inspection — 0.10.3 — measurements and labels, ligand neighborhoods, SES/SAS shape, numerical probes, planes/contours and profiles.

The application workspace

One Tools pane starts on the right at 480 px wide. Its four tabs are inside the pane; choose Position → Left to move it across the scene. Drag the inner divider to resize it. The existing header, file buttons, viewport toolbar and footer stay in their familiar places.

AreaWhat you do there
Top barRead the document title and unsaved indicator; choose Open, Convert, or Save. Three compact buttons show/hide Tools, enter Zen and toggle fullscreen.
Molecule tabMolecular data: counts, representation and biological controls. Selections and measurements: ordered picks, named sets, labels and neighborhoods. Provenance: source decisions and warnings.
Fields tabQuantum fields: orbitals, densities and sampled fields. Field rendering: volume/raycast, isovalue, resolution and mesh generation. Quantitative field inspection: probes, true planes, contours and line profiles.
Surfaces tabMolecular surfaces: coordinate-derived SES/SAS and live saved appearance. Saved surfaces: quantum/imported mesh visibility and triangle counts.
Appearance tabAppearance: field colors/opacity, surface shading and molecular AO. Atom colours: element palettes. Surface shader: custom GLSL sources, targets and live parameters.
Central sceneRotate, pan, zoom, and pick an atom or saved surface. Inspection information appears below the scene.
Footer and progress panelRead operation status, completed sampling slices, signed meshing passes, elapsed time, and errors.

Close Tools to give its width back to the scene. The header's Show tools button or Shift+T brings back the same pane. At narrow widths it overlays the scene instead of squeezing it. Switching tabs, changing side or hiding the pane retains tool drafts and the current camera; a hidden pane does not cancel a calculation.

Keyboard and widget controls

Use Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Left/Right and Home/End move among the four pane tabs. A widget heading is a button: activate it to collapse or expand that section. Collapsing retains settings and selections. In Safari on macOS, use Option-Tab if ordinary Tab skips buttons, or enable macOS Keyboard Navigation.

  1. To move a widget, drag its Reorder handle onto the upper or lower half of another heading in the same tab.
  2. Alternatively, click or tap the handle and choose Move up or Move down.
  3. With keyboard focus on the handle, use Up/Down to move one place, or Home/End to move to the start/end of that tab's stack. Focus stays on the handle and the new position is announced.
  4. Escape closes the move controls and returns focus to the handle.

Widget order stays within its tab. Pane side, width, active tab and visibility are profile preferences, alongside widget order/expansion; they are not part of a molecular document. Without profile storage they last for the session. On the focused divider, use Left/Right, Shift+arrows for a larger step, or Home/End to resize. Closing a widget differs from closing the whole pane: only closing the pane reclaims its scene width.

Shift+Z enters Zen: header, footer and Tools hide while Exit Zen, Tools, source identity and essential scene controls remain. Choose Tools to leave Zen and reopen the pane. Fullscreen is separate and uses the browser's actual facility. While fullscreen is active the platform handles Escape first; after leaving fullscreen, Escape can leave Zen. Visible exits remain available. Shortcuts leave editable inputs and modal dialogs alone. The orbital browser remains a modal chooser.

Progress and Cancel calculation remain available outside the pane, including in Zen, for cancellable work. Hiding Tools or switching tabs neither restarts nor cancels it. A noncancellable Save shows status without a misleading Cancel action. Browser fullscreen does not establish native macOS fullscreen or multi-monitor qualification.

The scene's Zoom in, Zoom out, Fit scene, and Reset view buttons provide alternatives to mouse gestures. See scene navigation.

What is saved?

Save writes the molecular document. It does not capture every view preference. The phrase Saved surfaces means meshes in the current document's memory; they reach disk only when you save the document.

Kind of stateExamplesAfter reopening or restarting
Saved molecular dataAtoms and biological identities, bonds, supplied basis/orbitals/density matrices or original sampled grids, provenance, saved meshes, named selections, measurements/labels and quantitative analysis definitions/caches.Retained in .eigenvista by a matching current build.
Saved document appearanceGlobal molecular representation, surface colors, opacity, and saved-mesh visibility.Retained in .eigenvista.
Session display settingsCamera, render mode, background, AO, atom palette, surface shading, shader/uniforms, preview quality, biological filters/styles, orbital checkboxes, and preview sampling cache.Not stored in the molecular file. Reapply after restarting; some settings continue across Open within a session.
Profile preferencesPane side, width, active tab and visibility; per-tab widget order and expansion.Remembered on that browser/desktop profile when storage is available; not shared with the document. Zen is transient.

Each mesh records the grid used to generate it, but the Grid resolution control resets in a new application session. Generated orbital/density grids are transient; their analytic inputs and saved meshes persist. Imported cube grids retain their original values. See saving and sharing and field caching.

When something looks wrong

SymptomNext step
Generate surfaces is disabledSelect an orbital, density matrix, or sampled field in Quantum fields. Wait for another active operation to finish or cancel it.
Generate selected is disabledCheck between 1 and 32 orbitals and enter a finite positive isovalue.
Raycast/volume choices are disabledSelect an available field and finish/cancel other active work. Analytic orbital/density previews do not require meshes.
No field appearsUse Fit scene, check the selected mode and field, and try a smaller positive isovalue. Read any preview error. An all-zero field has no nonzero surface.
A structure seems missingFit the scene, try Ball and stick, and check counts and biological visibility filters. Show all components clears those filters.
Atom/bond counts differ from the sourceRead Provenance: model, alternate, occupancy, unknown-element, declared-connection, and inferred-bond policies can change the selected display structure.
A calculation is slow or memory allocation failsRead the phase, counts and elapsed time. Cancel if needed. If the computer cannot allocate the requested data, retry with fewer orbitals, a lower grid resolution or a smaller explicit selection. Existing completed results are retained on recoverable errors.
Volume changes unexpectedly with qualityUse 0.5.14 or later; earlier builds had an opacity/quality interaction. In all versions, volume intersections with opaque objects remain approximate.
A file is rejectedRead the exact error and supported profile. Export an appropriate supported file rather than removing producer, units, or validation markers.
WebGL2 is unavailable / graphics context lostCheck graphics acceleration. A lost context requires restart/reload; save if possible beforehand. Unsaved data can be lost.

See supported file formats for import options and rendering limits for guidance on sampled previews and transparency.

For a problem report, include the package filename or BUILD-INFO.json, Mac model/processor, OS version, exact error, steps, and a screenshot. Share a small input only if you have permission to publish it. Keep confidential research data out of public issues.
