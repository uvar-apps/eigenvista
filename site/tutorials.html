Before you start

These GUI instructions follow EigenVista 0.10.1 and its four-tab Tools pane. The 0.10.1 Mac download includes every workflow below: molecular display, quantum rendering, measurements, SES/SAS and quantitative analysis. Open example files with the app’s Open button. The website supplies instructions and data; the app runs the local workflows.

Start with the first lesson if you are new to the viewport. Then choose the kind of data you work with. Keep the original inputs and save your work to new .eigenvista files.

Your learning path

  1. Open, inspect, and save your first molecule. Use three-atom water to learn rotation, zoom, picking, representations, and native Save.

  2. Explore orbitals from a real calculation. Explore the HOMO and LUMO of a bicyclic hydrocarbon, compare volume and surface views, and save a smooth orbital mesh.

  3. Inspect a sampled cube field. Explore a scalar field around a 29-atom complex, compare volume and surface views, and save the grid with its mesh.

  4. Read a large biological structure. Use MatterViz Pastel and ambient occlusion on 1AON, then switch to chain cartoons and isolate a chain.

Explore with your AI agent

The AI connector guide explains how to use Codex, Claude Code, Pi, OpenCode or another local coding agent with the installed viewer and its bundled helper. EigenVista 0.10.1 for Mac includes everything needed to connect the viewer to your agent; complete the setup guide before starting these lessons.

  1. From a prompt to a HOMO figure: a recorded orbital, shader and image-export session.
  2. Explore electron density: direct volume sampling, an envelope mesh and a view into the molecular interior.
  3. Read crowded atomic structure: ambient occlusion and atomic representations on a large molecular assembly.
  4. Find the assembly's shape: trace, tube, ribbon and cartoon views, with atomic detail when you need it.

Measure shape and quantitative fields

These three lessons use the scientific-inspection tools in EigenVista 0.10.1. They are included in the 0.10.1 Mac download. They can be followed in the GUI or by giving the ordinary-language prompts to a connected agent.
  1. Measure, label and keep the geometry: ordered picks, a real carbon–carbon distance, torsions, annotated PNG/CSV and a ligand-neighborhood extension.
  2. Compare solvent-accessible and excluded shape: explicit radii/probe/spacing, SAS versus SES, shader targets and cached reopen.
  3. Inspect an orbital quantitatively: probes, a true plane with contours, fixed units/range, bond profiles and exported numerical evidence.

The scientific-inspection manual explains the controls, scientific meaning, save/export behavior and limits.

How to use the checkpoints

Each lesson gives an expected result before the next action. Wait for the footer’s completed result when a calculation is running. A calculation’s live phase percentage is not a whole-job completion indicator. Cancel retains previously completed surfaces.

If your controls differ, check the installed version, choose the pane tab and expand the relevant widget. In the current source, Molecule holds structure and measurements, Fields holds quantum rendering and quantitative inspection, Surfaces holds molecular shape and saved meshes, and Appearance holds palettes, AO and shaders. Use Fit scene if an object appears missing. Tour the workspace or browse the real application captures.
